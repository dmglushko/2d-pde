HOW TO READ QUALITY

Mean-ratio quality = 4√3·|area| / (sum of squared edge lengths). An equilateral triangle scores 1; a collapsed triangle scores 0.

Minimum angle is the smallest corner angle. Small angles often indicate a thin triangle.

Edge ratio = longest edge / shortest edge. Values near 1 are more balanced. These measures use the three corner nodes for P1 and P2.

Signed area is positive for counterclockwise node order and negative for clockwise order. Let tolerance = 10⁻¹⁴ × bounding-box span². A triangle is degenerate when |twice signed area| ≤ tolerance and inverted when twice signed area < −tolerance.
